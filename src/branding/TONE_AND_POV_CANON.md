# TONE & POV CANON (v1.0)
**Status**: LOCKED
**Date**: 2026-01-30

> This is the non-negotiable operating system for everything that follows. If something conflicts with this later, the later thing is wrong.

## 1. WHO WE ARE (AND WHO WE ARE NOT)

**We are:**
*   Operators who have sat in the seat.
*   Calm, structured thinkers at inflection points.
*   External perspective that respects internal competence.

**We are not:**
*   Gurus.
*   Tool vendors.
*   AI evangelists.
*   Efficiency consultants.
*   “Growth hackers”.

## 2. OUR CORE BELIEF
> "Growth doesn’t stall because teams aren’t working hard — it stalls because the way growth decisions get made hasn’t evolved with scale."

Everything must ladder to this.

## 3. HOW WE TALK ABOUT THE CUSTOMER
**We assume:**
*   They are competent.
*   They have already succeeded.
*   They know their business better than we do.
*   They are not “missing basics”.

**We never say or imply:**
*   “You’re doing it wrong”
*   “You lack strategy”
*   “You need education”

**We do say:**
*   “What worked earlier starts to strain”
*   “The business has changed shape”
*   “The operating model hasn’t caught up yet”

## 4. PROBLEM LANGUAGE (NO SCARE TACTICS)
**Allowed:**
*   “Feels harder than it should”
*   “Less clarity”
*   “More explanation required”
*   “Noise creeping in”
*   “Quiet stalls”

**Not allowed:**
*   “Broken”
*   “Failing”
*   “Bleeding”
*   “Chaos”
*   “Disaster”
*   “Everything is wrong”

## 5. OUR ROLE
**We are not the hero. The customer is the hero.**

**We are:**
*   The mirror.
*   The system.
*   The outside judgment.

**Language test:**
*   If the copy makes GTM-360 sound smarter than the customer: **FAIL**.
*   If it makes the customer feel smarter after reading: **PASS**.

## 6. AI & PRODUCT POSTURE
AI is never the headline. AI is infrastructure.

**We never say:**
*   “AI-powered growth”
*   “AI-driven magic”
*   “Autonomous revenue”

**We say:**
*   “Signals”
*   “Evidence”
*   “Judgment”
*   “Decision support”

## 7. THE SIGNATURE PLANNING CYCLE (CANON)
This stays exactly as is. No renaming.

1.  **Where are we?**
2.  **How did we get here?**
3.  **Where could we be?**
4.  **How do we get there?**
5.  **Are we getting there?**

## 8. CALL-TO-ACTION PHILOSOPHY
CTAs should feel like next steps, not conversions.

**Good:**
*   Start here
*   Have a conversation
*   See how we work
*   Explore the agents

**Bad:**
*   Book a demo
*   Get started now
*   Optimize your revenue
*   Unlock growth

## 9. WHAT “GREAT” FEELS LIKE
*   Founders feel seen, not sold to.
*   CROs feel relieved, not judged.
*   RevOps leaders feel validated, not demoted.
*   The product feels like a natural extension of the thinking.

## 10. v2 — THE OPERATOR SYSTEM (addendum, 2026-08-24)

The product suite (Compass · Cockpit · Agent Portal) extends the method instead of replacing it.
Reconciles the "AI is never the headline" rule with the system era:

*   **The system IS the method, operationalized.** Not a product that replaces operators — the method they already trust, running continuously between engagements.
*   **AI is the engine, never the hero.** We say "keeps the method running", "executes the support work", "only acts on your confirmation". We never say "AI-powered growth", "autonomous revenue", or "let AI run it".
*   **The customer stays the hero.** The operator diagnoses and designs; the system executes the routine work; the human confirms every move. "The strategy you approve runs itself. You confirm the moves."
*   **Product copy obeys section 4.** No "broken/chaos/disaster". Stalled, straining, noise creeping in — not crisis language.
*   **Naming is fixed:** Compass (set the course) · Cockpit (command the execution) · Agent Portal (specialists on call). Parent: **GTM-360 — The Revenue Operating System.**
*   **Test:** if the copy implies the software outsmarts the customer's operator — FAIL. If it implies the operator's judgment is what the software amplifies — PASS.

## 11. v3 — THE REVENUE LOOP (addendum, 2026-09-20)

The offer is now a strategy firm whose deliverable is a working system that keeps running. Reconciles the consulting identity with the agentic system into one story:

*   **The business model:** GTM-360 is a GTM strategy firm. We bring operator judgment and thought leadership (the strategy layer), develop the playbooks and process ownership (the Method layer), then set up the agentic system that runs the end-to-end GTM process with defined handoffs (the engine layer). Sold as: Diagnose → Build → Run → Grow.
*   **The thesis (use everywhere):** "We build your revenue system. Then it keeps running." Most advisories hand you a roadmap and leave. We build the loop — and it keeps working after we do.
*   **The model is the bowtie, not a list.** Strategy (top envelope) → Attract → Convert → Grow (the customer journey) → Operations (bottom envelope) → the loop closes (Enhance) back to Strategy. The graphic is the signature; recognition through repetition.
*   **The customer journey is the story; the org chart is the label.** Engines carry functional names on the surface (Strategy / Marketing / Sales / Expansion / Operations) because buyers navigate by function. The journey language (Attract / Convert / Grow) is the story that sells. No engine is ever named "Understand" or "Prove."
*   **Validate, not prove.** The bottom envelope validates the numbers before you act; the loop enhances next quarter. Never "prove".
*   **Visual-first (show, don't scroll).** Every claim gets a video, a diagram, a table, a chart, or a number. Prose is connective tissue, not the content. One idea per viewport. Tools placed where they carry the argument — never force-fit.
*   **Real quotes only.** Expert quotes are real, attributed, and placed where they carry the argument (e.g. the Bill Gates "automation magnifies" line on the process-first argument). Never invented attribution.
*   **Humanized consultant+operator voice.** A consultant who has sat in the seat: calm, direct, respects the customer's competence. No machine tells, no forced triads, no "It's not X it's Y", no one-line closers, varied rhythm, specific over vague. The customer is the hero; we are the mirror and the system.
*   **Base-level engine + roadmap posture.** The loop ships at its base with every engagement; the roadmap is a forward capability progression ("grows next"), never "planned / demo / coming soon". Never defensive, never shallow.
*   **Naming (expanded):** Compass (goals &amp; governance) · Cockpit (deals &amp; the book) · Agent Portal (specialists on call) · Content (the CREATE engine, part of Marketing) · Method (the operating model). Parent: **GTM-360 — The Revenue Operating System.**
